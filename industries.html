How We Serve Different Sectors

Broadcast

For television and radio broadcasters, our AI solutions ensure content compliance, precise ad verification, and high-quality subtitle delivery. We help streamline operations, reduce manual errors, and provide actionable insights into audience engagement and competitive landscape.

  • Automated subtitle QC and compliance
  • Real-time ad and promo spot detection
  • Competitive content monitoring
  • Audience sentiment analysis

OTT & Streaming

Over-the-top (OTT) platforms benefit from our solutions by maintaining content quality, managing global content libraries with ease, and optimizing ad monetization. Our AI ensures a superior viewer experience and maximizes revenue potential.

  • Multilingual content processing and localization
  • Automated content tagging and metadata generation
  • Dynamic ad insertion verification
  • Viewer engagement analytics

Radio

For radio networks and advertisers, tabsons.ai provides advanced monitoring of broadcasts, turning spoken content into actionable data. Track mentions, analyze ad placements, and understand listener sentiment to refine programming and campaigns.

  • Real-time speech-to-text transcription
  • Brand and keyword mention tracking
  • Competitive ad monitoring on radio
  • Audience engagement and trend analysis

Advertising & PR Agencies

Agencies rely on tabsons.ai for precise campaign monitoring, competitor analysis, and media landscape insights. Our tools help validate campaign effectiveness, track brand mentions across media, and inform strategic planning for clients.

  • Brand reputation and sentiment monitoring
  • Automated media reporting

Corporate & Brands

Corporations and brands utilize our media intelligence for real-time reputation management, crisis monitoring, and understanding their brand's presence across all media types. Gain critical insights to protect and enhance your brand image.
