About tabsons.ai

Pioneering the future of media intelligence with cutting-edge AI technology and an unwavering commitment to innovation.

Who We Are

Tabsons is a trusted name in media and satcom products, specializing in system integration for media, broadcast, and IT services. Our 24/7 infrastructure ensures seamless operations, supporting over 600+ channels across India. With 40 million hours of media monitoring expertise, we deliver deep analytics for informed decision-making.

Our innovative Tabsons.AI Application Studio drives AI-powered solutions that enhance operational efficiency and system performance. By harnessing the power of AI, we deliver intelligent solutions that cater to the growing needs of the media and technology sectors. With an impressive 99.5% accuracy in AI applications like brand detection and speech analytics, we set industry benchmarks.

At Tabsons, we are dedicated to pushing the boundaries of technology and leveraging AI to transform the media landscape, ensuring we remain a trusted partner in this rapidly evolving industry.

Our Vision
